# Zombie Hockey League — Build Brief

**The match:** The Montreal Cadaviens host the Detroit Dread Wings in one arcade match on one rink: 3 periods of 3 minutes. Most goals at the final whistle wins. A tie goes to 2 minutes of sudden-death overtime, then a shootout.

**The season:** Eight zombie teams play each other once. A win earns 2 points; an overtime or shootout win earns 1; a loss earns 0. The top two meet in a one-match final. One match ships first; the season table follows.

**The 30-second loop:** Skate to the puck, pass or carry up the rink, shoot or check, score or lose the puck, reset. Every 30 seconds must hold skating, a pass or shot, and a check or scoring chance.

**Zombie rules:** No offsides, no icing, no stamina — the dead never tire. A checked zombie is flattened for 2 seconds and drops the puck.

**Controls:** Gamepad first — left stick skates, X passes, A shoots, B checks, Y switches skater, Start pauses. Keyboard fallback: WASD, Space, J, K, Tab, Esc.

**The line:** One 9-minute zombie-hockey match — no managing, no tower-building, no story — just skate, check, and score.
